How it works

2 m

  1. Stand back

    About two metres from your laptop, so the camera can see all of you.

  2. Match the gold figure on the beat

    Reach, hold, release. Every move lands on the music.

  3. The music only plays while your form holds

    Drop the pose and the melody drops out. Find it again and it comes back.
